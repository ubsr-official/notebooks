## 📌 CORE CONCEPTS

### **Nationalism**

A sense of **collective identity** and **shared history** that binds people together as a nation.

### **Nation vs Nation-State**

* **Nation** : A group with common identity, culture, history, language
* **Nation-State** : A nation with its own **sovereign government** and **defined territory**
* Before 19th century: Multi-national **dynastic empires** ruled (not nation-states)
* After 19th century: Rise of **nation-states** where citizens shared common identity

### **Key Transformation**

**Multi-national Empires** → **Nation-States**
(Kings ruled over diverse peoples) → (Citizens with shared identity governed)

## 🔥 THE FRENCH REVOLUTION & BIRTH OF NATIONALISM (1789)

### **Cause-Effect Chain:**

```
Absolute Monarchy in France 
         ↓
French Revolution (1789)
         ↓
Sovereignty transfers: KING → PEOPLE
         ↓
"Nation" = The People (not the monarch)
         ↓
BIRTH OF NATIONALISM
```

### **Revolutionary Measures Creating Collective Identity:**

| Measure                                                        | Purpose                    | Effect                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| **La patrie**(fatherland) &**le citoyen**(citizen) | Emphasize united community | Created sense of belonging |
| **Tricolour flag**                                       | Replace royal standard     | Common national symbol     |
| **Estates General**→**National Assembly**         | Democratic representation  | People's sovereignty       |
| New**hymns, oaths, martyrs**                             | National pride             | Emotional connection       |
| **Centralized administration**                           | Uniform laws for all       | Equality across territory  |
| Abolished**internal customs duties**                     | Economic unity             | Easier trade               |
| Uniform**weights & measures**                            | Standardization            | National integration       |
| **French language**promoted                              | Paris French = standard    | Cultural unity             |

### **💡 LOGIC: Why This Created Nationalism**

∵ Revolution made **people** (not king) the source of power

∴ People needed **common identity** to act as one nation

∴ Common symbols, language, laws = **collective consciousness**

∴ **Nationalism born**

## ⚔️ NAPOLEONIC ERA (1797-1815)

### **Napoleon's Dual Impact:**

#### **POSITIVE REFORMS** ✓

1. **Napoleonic Code (1804)** :

* Abolished privileges based on **birth**
* **Equality before law**
* Right to **property** secured

1. **Administrative Rationalization** :

* Simplified divisions
* Abolished **feudal system**
* Freed peasants from **serfdom** and **manorial dues**
* Removed **guild restrictions** in towns
* Improved **transport & communication**

1. **Who Benefited** : Peasants, artisans, businessmen, middle class

#### **NEGATIVE REACTIONS** ✗

1. **Increased taxation**
2. **Censorship**
3. **Forced conscription** into French armies
4. **Loss of political freedom**

### **Cause-Effect Logic:**

```
Initial Welcome (Holland, Switzerland, Brussels, Milan, Warsaw)
         ↓
Saw French as "liberators" from monarchy
         ↓
BUT: Reforms ≠ Political Freedom
         ↓
Administrative changes + Oppression
         ↓
Welcome → HOSTILITY
         ↓
Growth of Anti-French Nationalism
```

### **🔗 Connect the Dots:**

 **Irony** : Napoleon **spread** nationalism while trying to **conquer** Europe

→ His reforms showed benefits of unified systems

→ But his oppression made people want **their own** nation-states

→  **Unintended consequence** : Strengthened nationalism against him

## 🏛️ CONSERVATIVE REACTION: VIENNA CONGRESS (1815)

### **Conservatism Defined:**

* Preserve  **traditional institutions** : Monarchy, Church, social hierarchy
* But **modernize** them (not return to pre-1789)
* **Strong autocratic state** with modern army & bureaucracy

### **Vienna Congress Objectives:**

```
Napoleon Defeated (1815)
         ↓
European Powers Meet: Britain, Russia, Prussia, Austria
         ↓
Host: Metternich (Austrian Chancellor)
         ↓
GOAL: Undo Napoleonic changes + Restore monarchies
```

### **Vienna Settlement - Key Decisions:**

| Action                                                                         | Purpose                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| **Bourbon dynasty restored**in France                                    | Undo revolution          |
| France loses**annexed territories**                                      | Weaken France            |
| **Buffer states**around France (Netherlands + Belgium, Genoa + Piedmont) | Prevent French expansion |
| Prussia gains**western territories**                                     | Balance of power         |
| Austria controls**northern Italy**                                       | Austrian influence       |
| Russia gets part of**Poland**                                            | Reward ally              |
| German Confederation of**39 states**kept                                 | No unified Germany       |

### **Repressive Measures Post-1815:**

* **Autocratic regimes** established
* **Censorship** of newspapers, books, plays, songs
* No **criticism or dissent** tolerated
* **Freedom of press** denied

### **💡 LOGIC: Why This Failed Long-Term**

∵ Can't suppress **ideas** of liberty and nationalism forever

∴ Underground movements emerged

∴ Revolutions became inevitable

## 🔴 THE REVOLUTIONARIES

### **Secret Societies**

* Underground networks to **train revolutionaries**
* Spread ideas of **liberty, freedom, nation-states**
* Opposed **monarchies** established at Vienna

### **Giuseppe Mazzini (1805-1872)**

**Profile:**

* Born in  **Genoa** , Italy
* Member of **Carbonari** (secret society)
* Exiled in **1831** for revolutionary activities
* Founded:
  * **Young Italy** (Marseilles)
  * **Young Europe** (Berne) - for Poland, France, Italy, Germany

**Vision:**

* **God intended nations** to be natural units
* Italy must be **unified republic** (not patchwork)
* Opposed **monarchy**
* Wanted **democratic republics**

**Impact:**

* Metternich called him **"most dangerous enemy of social order"**
* Inspired nationalist movements across Europe
* Model for secret societies in other countries

### **ROMANTICISM & CULTURAL NATIONALISM**

#### **What Was Romanticism?**

* Cultural movement
* **Criticized** : Glorification of reason & science (Enlightenment)
* **Focused** : Emotions, intuition, mystical feelings
* **Goal** : Create sense of **shared heritage** as basis of nation

#### **Key Romantics:**

**Johann Gottfried Herder (1744-1803)**

* German philosopher
* **Das volk** (the common people) = true German culture
* **Volksgeist** (spirit of nation) in folk songs, poetry, dances
* **Collecting folklore** = nation-building

**Grimm Brothers (Jacob & Wilhelm)**

* Collected **folktales** from villages (1812)
* Published fairy tales (33-volume German dictionary too)
* Saw French domination as threat to **German culture**
* Folktales = **pure German spirit**

#### **Role of Language:**

**Case Study: Poland**

* Partitioned by Russia, Prussia, Austria (18th century)
* No independent territory BUT nationalism alive through:
  * **Music** : Karol Kurpinski (operas, folk dances - polonaise, mazurka)
  * **Language** : After Russian occupation, Polish banned
  * Clergy used Polish in Church
  * Religious instruction in Polish
  * Priests jailed/sent to Siberia
  * **Polish = symbol of resistance**

### **🔗 Connect the Dots:**

**Culture = Political Tool**

→ Shared language, folklore, music = **common identity**

→ Common identity = **basis for nationalism**

→ Even without territory, **culture kept nation alive**

### **1848: THE REVOLUTIONARY YEAR**

#### **Economic Crisis → Popular Revolt**

**Background (1830s-1840s):**

* **Population explosion** across Europe
* More job seekers than jobs
* Rural → Urban migration = **overcrowded slums**
* **Cheap English imports** hurt small producers
* Peasants under **feudal dues**
* **Food prices rising**

**1848 Triggers:**

* **Food shortages**
* **Widespread unemployment**

#### **France (1848):**

```
Paris population on streets
         ↓
Barricades erected
         ↓
Louis Philippe flees
         ↓
National Assembly proclaims REPUBLIC
         ↓
Suffrage to all adult males (21+)
         ↓
Right to work guaranteed
         ↓
National workshops for employment
```

#### **Silesia Weavers' Revolt (1845):**

* **Contractors** reduced payments drastically
* Weavers' **extreme misery**
* June 4: Weavers march to contractor's mansion
* Demand **higher wages**
* Met with **scorn**
* Smashed property, plundered storehouse
* Army called → **11 weavers shot**

## 🇩🇪 UNIFICATION OF GERMANY (1866-1871)

### **Shift in Nationalism:**

 **1848** : Democracy + Nationalism (Liberal)

 **Post-1848** : Nationalism + **State Power** (Conservative)

### **Why Middle-Class Failed (1848)?**

* Opposed by **monarchy + military + Junkers** (large landowners)
* Too weak alone

### **Prussia's Role:**

#### **Otto von Bismarck - The Architect**

* Chief Minister of Prussia
* Policy: **"Blood and Iron"** (military force, not parliament)
* Used **Prussian army + bureaucracy**

#### **Three Wars → Unification:**

| Year              | War                 | Outcome          |
| ----------------- | ------------------- | ---------------- |
| **1864**    | vs**Denmark** | Prussian victory |
| **1866**    | vs**Austria** | Prussian victory |
| **1870-71** | vs**France**  | Prussian victory |

```
Three Prussian Victories
         ↓
January 18, 1871
         ↓
Hall of Mirrors, Palace of Versailles
         ↓
German princes + army + ministers gather
         ↓
King Wilhelm I of Prussia proclaimed GERMAN EMPEROR (Kaiser)
         ↓
UNIFIED GERMANY CREATED
```

### **Character of German Unification:**

* **Top-down** (not bottom-up like liberals wanted)
* **Prussian dominance** (not equal partnership)
* **Military power** created unity (not people's will)
* **Modernization** : Currency, banking, legal systems standardized
* Prussian model = **template for Germany**

### **💡 Logic:**

∵ Liberal democratic path failed (1848)

∴ Conservative **military path** succeeded

∴ Germany unified BUT under **authoritarian system**

∴ Nationalism divorced from democracy

## 🇮🇹 UNIFICATION OF ITALY (1859-1870)

### **Starting Situation:**

* Italy divided into **7 states**
* Only **Sardinia-Piedmont** ruled by Italian prince
* North: **Austrian Habsburgs**
* Center: **Pope**
* South: **Bourbon kings of Spain**
* No common **Italian language** form

### **Failed Earlier Attempts:**

* Mazzini's **Young Italy** (1830s)
* Revolutions of **1831** and **1848** both failed

### **Successful Strategy - Two Tracks:**

#### **Track 1: Cavour's Diplomacy**

**Count Camillo de Cavour**

* Chief Minister of **Sardinia-Piedmont**
* Not revolutionary, not democrat
* Spoke **French better than Italian** (elite background)
* **Strategy** : Diplomatic alliance with France

```
Alliance with France
         ↓
1859: Defeated Austria
         ↓
Austrian forces driven from North
```

#### **Track 2: Garibaldi's Military Campaign**

**Giuseppe Garibaldi (1807-82)**

* From coastal trading family, **sailor**
* Met Mazzini (1833), joined **Young Italy**
* Exiled to South America (1834-1848)
* **1854** : Supported Victor Emmanuel II
* **1860** : Led **Expedition of the Thousand** to South Italy

```
Red Shirts (Garibaldi's volunteers)
         ↓
Marched into South Italy + Kingdom of Two Sicilies
         ↓
Won peasant support
         ↓
Drove out Spanish rulers
```

### **Unification Timeline:**

| Year              | Region                    | How                                          |
| ----------------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **1858-60** | Sardinia-Piedmont base    | Starting point                               |
| **1860**    | South Italy, Two Sicilies | Garibaldi's campaign                         |
| **1866**    | Venetia                   | Allied with Prussia vs Austria               |
| **1870**    | Papal States (Rome)       | French troops withdrew (Franco-Prussian war) |

 **1861** : Victor Emmanuel II proclaimed **King of United Italy**

### **Irony:**

* Most Italians didn't know about "liberal-nationalist ideology"
* Southern peasants who supported Garibaldi never heard of "Italia"
* Thought **"La Talia"** was Victor Emmanuel's wife!

### **💡 Logic:**

∵ Mazzini's **revolutionary path** failed

∴ **Diplomatic + Military** combination succeeded

∴ Cavour's tactics + Garibaldi's military = **Unified Italy**

**BUT** unification was  **elite-driven** , not mass movement

## 🇬🇧 BRITAIN: THE EXCEPTIONAL CASE

### **Why Britain Was Different:**

| Aspect                             | Rest of Europe        | Britain                               |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| **Process**                  | Sudden revolution/war | **Long, gradual process**       |
| **Nation pre-18th century?** | No unified nations    | **No British nation**either     |
| **Primary identities**       | Regional/ethnic       | **English, Welsh, Scot, Irish** |
| **How unified?**             | Revolution/war        | **English dominance**extended   |

### **The Process:**

#### **1. England's Growing Power**

* Wealth, importance, power increased
* Extended influence over other British Isles nations

#### **2. Role of Parliament**

* **1688** : English Parliament seized power from monarchy
* Became instrument of **nation-state formation**
* England at center

#### **3. Act of Union (1707)**

```
England + Scotland
         ↓
"United Kingdom of Great Britain"
         ↓
BUT: England imposed influence on Scotland
         ↓
British Parliament dominated by ENGLISH members
```

#### **4. Suppression of Scotland**

* Scottish culture **systematically suppressed**
* **Catholic clans** in Highlands: Terrible repression
* **Forbidden** : Gaelic language, national dress
* **Forcibly driven** from homeland

#### **5. Ireland Incorporated (1801)**

* Deeply divided: **Catholics vs Protestants**
* English helped **Protestants dominate** Catholics
* **Wolfe Tone's** United Irishmen revolt (1798): Failed
* **Forcibly incorporated** into UK (1801)

### **Creating "British" Identity:**

* **Propagation** of dominant English culture
* New  **symbols** :
  * **Union Jack** (British flag)
  * **National anthem** ("God Save Our Noble King")
  * **English language** promoted
* Older nations = **subordinate partners**

### **💡 Logic:**

∵ No revolution → **gradual state formation**

∴ Through **parliamentary power** and **cultural dominance**

∴ Ethnic identities  **suppressed** , not celebrated

∴ "British" nation = **imposed** unity, not organic growth

## 🔗 CONNECTING THE DOTS

### **Why Did French Revolution Create Nationalism?**

1. **Power shift** : King → People
2. **People need identity** to act as unified nation
3. **Common symbols/language/laws** created collective consciousness
4. **First time** "nation" = "the people" (not the ruler)

### **Why Did Napoleon Both Spread and Unite Europe Against Himself?**

1. His **reforms** showed benefits of unified, rational systems
2. But **oppression** made people want freedom
3. **Result** : They wanted unified systems BUT in **their own nation-states**
4. **Irony** : He spread the very nationalism that defeated him

### **Why Did Liberal Revolutions of 1848 Fail?**

1. **Aristocracy + military** too powerful
2. **Middle class** lost support of **workers/artisans**
3. **Divided opposition** vs **united conservative forces**
4. **But** : Ideas survived; monarchs had to make concessions

### **Why Did Germany and Italy Unify Differently From Liberal Dreams?**

1. **Liberal path failed** (1848)
2. **Military/diplomatic path** succeeded
3. **Germany** : Prussian military dominance (Bismarck)
4. **Italy** : Sardinia-Piedmont diplomacy (Cavour) + military (Garibaldi)
5. **Result** : Unification achieved BUT **without democracy**

### **Why Was British Nation-Formation Unique?**

1. **No revolution** needed
2. **Gradual process** through parliamentary power
3. **English dominance** extended over centuries
4. **Cultural suppression** (not celebration) of diversity
5. **State preceded nation** (opposite of continent)

### **How Did Nationalism Turn Into Imperialism?**

1. **Early** : Nationalism = self-determination, democracy
2. **Late 19th century** : Became aggressive, intolerant
3. **Used by states** for power expansion
4. **Combined with** : Imperial ambitions, big power rivalry
5. **Result** : Competition for colonies, territories
