## Section IV: Case-Based Questions (10 marks)

**Question 25:** Read the following case and answer the questions that follow:

**Case Study: Mountain Expedition**

Rahul, a fitness enthusiast from Delhi, joins a mountain expedition to climb a peak at 4,500 meters altitude. On the first day at base camp (3,000 meters), he experiences shortness of breath and fatigue despite being physically fit. His guide explains that this is normal and that his body will adapt over the next few days.

After a week of acclimatization, Rahul notices that:
- His breathing rate has decreased from the initial high rate
- He feels more energetic and can climb longer distances
- His appetite has improved
- He sleeps better at night

The guide explains that several physiological changes occur during acclimatization to high altitude.

**Questions:**

**25a.** Why did Rahul experience shortness of breath and fatigue initially? Explain the physiological reason. (2 marks)

**25b.** What changes occur in Rahul's body during acclimatization that help him adapt to high altitude? Mention any three specific adaptations. (3 marks)

**25c.** If Rahul had a pre-existing anemia (low hemoglobin), how would this condition affect his acclimatization process? Justify your answer. (3 marks)

**25d.** Explain why the breathing rate eventually decreases after the initial period of rapid breathing at high altitude. (2 marks)
