## Section IV: Case-Based Questions (10 marks)

**Question 25:** Read the following case and answer the questions that follow:

**Case Study: Comparative Physiology Investigation**

Dr. Sarah, a comparative physiologist, studies three animals adapted to different environments:
- **Animal A**: A deep-sea fish living at 2000m depth where water temperature is 4°C and pressure is 200 times atmospheric pressure
- **Animal B**: A desert mammal that rarely drinks water and can survive extreme temperatures (50°C during day, 10°C at night)
- **Animal C**: A high-altitude bird that migrates between sea level and 6000m altitude regularly

Each animal has evolved unique adaptations in their life processes to survive in their respective environments. Dr. Sarah's research focuses on understanding how nutrition, respiration, circulation, and excretion are modified in each species.

Her preliminary findings show:
- Animal A has specialized proteins that function under high pressure and low temperature
- Animal B produces highly concentrated urine and has modified kidney structure
- Animal C has enhanced oxygen-carrying capacity and efficient gas exchange systems

**Questions:**

**25a.** Analyze the specific respiratory challenges faced by Animal A and predict the adaptations in its respiratory and circulatory systems. (3 marks)

**25b.** Explain how Animal B's excretory system adaptations help it survive in its environment. What other life process modifications would support this adaptation? (3 marks)

**25c.** Compare the circulatory system requirements of Animal C at sea level versus high altitude. What physiological changes would occur during migration? (2 marks)

**25d.** If these three animals were placed in each other's environments, predict their survival challenges and explain why their specialized adaptations would become disadvantageous. (2 marks)
