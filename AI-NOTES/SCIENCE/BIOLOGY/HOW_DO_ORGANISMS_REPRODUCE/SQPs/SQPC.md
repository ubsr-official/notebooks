**15.** **Advanced Case Study: Multi-Species Reproductive Ecology in a Changing World**

Dr. Elena Rodriguez is leading an international research project studying the interconnected reproductive strategies of species in a biodiversity hotspot that is experiencing rapid environmental change due to deforestation, climate change, and pollution.

**The Ecosystem:**
- **Keystone Plant Species A**: Wind-pollinated, produces seeds annually, critical food source
- **Pollinator Species B**: Specialist pollinator for 15 plant species, temperature-sensitive reproduction
- **Predator Species C**: Controls herbivore populations, long gestation period, low reproductive rate
- **Herbivore Species D**: Primary consumer, reproduces rapidly when resources abundant

**Environmental Changes (Last 30 years):**
- Temperature increased by 3.5°C
- Rainfall patterns became erratic
- Forest fragmentation increased by 60%
- Pollution levels tripled
- Introduction of invasive Species E (generalist pollinator)

**Observed Reproductive Changes:**
- Species A: Reproductive failure increased from 10% to 45%
- Species B: Population declined 70%, reproduction now occurs 3 weeks earlier
- Species C: Reproductive success dropped 30%, delayed maturity by 1 year
- Species D: Population fluctuations increased dramatically
- Species E: Population exploded, now dominant pollinator for 8 plant species

**Genetic Analysis Results:**
- Species A shows reduced genetic diversity (30% loss in 30 years)
- Species B exhibits signs of inbreeding depression in fragmented populations
- Species C shows evidence of gene flow disruption between populations
- Hybrid vigor observed in Species A × related species crosses

**Questions:**

**(a)** Analyze the cascading effects of reproductive disruption in this ecosystem. How are the reproductive changes in each species interconnected, and what feedback loops might exist? (2 marks)

**(b)** Evaluate the potential evolutionary trajectories for each species over the next 50 years. Which species are most likely to adapt, which might go extinct, and what new ecological relationships might emerge? (2 marks)

**(c)** Design an evidence-based conservation intervention strategy that addresses the reproductive biology of all species simultaneously. Consider genetic management, habitat restoration, and potential assisted evolution approaches. (2 marks)
