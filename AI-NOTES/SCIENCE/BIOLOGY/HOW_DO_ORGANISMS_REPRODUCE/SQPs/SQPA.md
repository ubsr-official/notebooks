## SECTION D: CASE-BASED QUESTION [5 Marks]

**18.** **Case Study: Reproductive Strategies in Changing Environments**

A research team is studying two species of freshwater organisms:
- **Species A**: Reproduces only asexually through binary fission
- **Species B**: Can switch between asexual (budding) and sexual reproduction

The lake where these organisms live experiences seasonal changes:
- **Stable Season** (6 months): Consistent temperature, abundant food
- **Stress Season** (6 months): Temperature fluctuations, food scarcity, pollution

**Observations:**
- During stable seasons, both species thrive, but Species A reproduces faster
- During stress seasons, Species A population often crashes while Species B survives
- Species B switches to sexual reproduction during stress periods
- After stress periods, Species B shows greater diversity in traits

**Questions:**

**(a)** Explain why Species A reproduces faster during stable conditions. (1 mark)

**(b)** Analyze why Species A populations crash during stress periods while Species B survives. (2 marks)

**(c)** Predict what might happen to both species if the lake permanently shifted to unstable conditions due to climate change. Justify your prediction. (2 marks)
