# Set B: Chemical Reactions & Equations (40 Marks)

## Section A: Multiple Choice Questions (1 mark each) [8]
1. Which reaction requires light energy?
   a) Thermal decomposition of CaCO₃
   b) Decomposition of AgCl
   c) Combustion of CH₄
   d) Neutralization of HCl

2. In the redox reaction CuO + H₂ → Cu + H₂O, identify the oxidizing agent.
   a) CuO
   b) H₂
   c) Cu
   d) H₂O

3. For the reaction Fe + 2HCl → FeCl₂ + H₂, the type is:
   a) Combination
   b) Decomposition
   c) Displacement
   d) Double displacement

4. Which statement about exothermic reactions is true?
   a) ΔH > 0
   b) Heat is absorbed
   c) Products have lower energy than reactants
   d) They never occur spontaneously

5. In balancing Fe + O₂ → Fe₂O₃, the coefficient of O₂ is:
   a) 1
   b) 3/2
   c) 2
   d) 5/2

6. Which pair undergoes a double displacement reaction forming a precipitate?
   a) H₂ + Cl₂
   b) Zn + CuSO₄
   c) Pb(NO₃)₂ + KI
   d) C + O₂

7. Rancidity in oils is due to:
   a) Reduction
   b) Hydrolysis
   c) Oxidation
   d) Precipitation

8. A catalyst:
   a) Raises activation energy
   b) Is consumed by the reaction
   c) Provides alternative pathway
   d) Changes reaction equilibrium composition
